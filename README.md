## The idea

A tiny cheerful white robot with a glowing orange visor rides triumphantly
on top of a gigantic glowing lightbulb lifting off like a hot-air balloon
through a deep indigo dawn sky. The bulb blazes warm golden light with a
clean zig-zag filament; long bold ribbons of teal, magenta and gold stream
behind it; confetti and speed lines fill the sky; below, a small stylised
neon city glows at the bottom edge. One huge clear focal shape (the bulb)
dominates the frame. No text at all.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) resolves the output dir from
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), creates it if missing, and
copies the beside-the-script `entry.png` into it as `entry.png`.
Deterministic: two runs write byte-identical output.
